Hi, I’m Tobi,

Photography began as a way to document my travels and business trips.

It fascinates me due to the intersection of technology and art, which makes it easy to start and impossible to perfect.

Aperture defines depth of focus and shutter speed how much time is integrated into one frame. Composition comes next, and seems to be the most important one. Golden ratios, aspects of balance and imbalance, color, brightness and texture. All this is helpful to create a good photo, but it is not enough for a great one.

It is the stories and hidden details, which make it beautiful. Taking the time to find those moments, paying attention to the quiet details and capturing them is a great joy.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope these photographs encourage you to pause, look a little closer, and find beauty in the ordinary.
