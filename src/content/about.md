title: About me

Hi, I'm Tobi,

Photography began as a way to document my travels, but over time it became something much more. It taught me to slow down, pay attention, and appreciate the quiet details that often go unnoticed.

Most of my work focuses on landscapes, wildlife, street scenes, and architecture. Rather than chasing dramatic moments, I enjoy discovering beauty in everyday places, changing light, and fleeting encounters.

This portfolio is a collection of photographs from my journeys and personal projects. Each image reflects a moment that caught my attention and felt worth preserving.

Thank you for visiting, and I hope these photographs encourage you to pause, look a little closer, and find beauty in the ordinary.
